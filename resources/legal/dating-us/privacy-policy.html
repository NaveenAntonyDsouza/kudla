This Privacy Policy explains how {{ app_name }} ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you use our website and app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Profile information you provide: name, date of birth, gender, relationship status, faith and denomination, education, occupation, location, photographs, a description about yourself and what you are looking for in a partner.

  • Verification documents: identity documents you choose to upload so we can verify your profile.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create your profile and show it to other registered members

  • Process payments
  • Keep the community safe: verify profiles, prevent fraud and misuse, and respond to reports
  • Understand how the service is used and improve it
  • Comply with the law

3. How We Share Information

Your profile is visible to other registered members according to your privacy settings. We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the service, bound to use it only for that purpose: website hosting and email delivery, payment processors, Google (Firebase) for app notifications, and Google Analytics for website analytics. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the safety of our members. Our service is operated from India and your information may be processed outside the United States.

4. How Long We Keep Information

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your profile and delete or de-identify your personal information within 90 days, except where we must keep certain records longer by law (for example payment records).

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • View and edit your profile, control your photo privacy, hide your profile, and unsubscribe from emails at any time.
  • Delete your account and personal information.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold, get a copy, correct it, delete it, and opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information.

6. Cookies and Analytics

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences, and Google Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use {{ app_name }} — for example which pages are visited and whether registration was completed. You can block these cookies in your browser settings without affecting how the site works.
