# Kudla Matrimony — Staff Quick-Start Guide

Welcome! This is your one-page cheat-sheet plus step-by-step guides for your four daily jobs.
Keep it next to you for the first couple of weeks.

---

## 🔑 Logging in
1. Open **https://kudlamatrimony.com/console** in Chrome.
2. Enter the **email** and **password** you were given. Change your password after the first login (top-right menu → Profile).
3. You'll land on the **Dashboard**. Your main workspace is **User Management → All Members**.

> You'll only see the menus you're allowed to use. If something isn't on your screen, it's not part of your role — that's normal.

---

## 🧭 The Members page — your home base
**User Management → All Members.** The row of **tabs** along the top filters the list, and each shows a live count:

| Tab | What it shows | You act on it when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending Approval** | New profiles awaiting your approval | every morning — clear this first |
| **No Photo** | Members with no photo | call/message them to add one |
| **Incomplete** | Profiles below 60% complete | help them finish their details |
| **Recent (7 days)** | New sign-ups this week | welcome + onboard them |
| **All Members** | Everyone | general search & lookup |

Use the **🔍 search box** (name, Matri ID, phone, or email) to find any specific member fast.

## 📋 Your 4 jobs — step by step

### SOP 1 — Approve a new profile
1. Go to the **Pending Approval** tab.
2. Click a member to **open their profile** and check the details look genuine (name, photo, age, contact).
3. Click **Approve**. Confirm.
4. ✅ They're now live and visible to other members.
> If something looks fake/spam, **don't approve** — flag it to the owner, or use **Suspend** (see the back page).

### SOP 2 — Log a follow-up / call note
1. Find the member (search or a tab).
2. On their row, click **Notes**.
3. Pick the **type** (Call / WhatsApp / Email / Meeting / Walk-in), type what happened, and set a **Follow-up date** if you need to contact them again.
4. **Save.** Your note (with your name + time) is now on their record.
> Your follow-ups appear in **Reports → Follow-up Report** so nothing slips through. Check it daily.

### SOP 3 — Register a walk-in / phone enquiry
1. On the Members page, click **Register on Behalf** (top-right).
2. Fill in **Full name, Gender, Date of birth, Email, Phone**. Leave the password blank to auto-generate one.
3. Tick **Send credentials via email** so they get their login.
4. **Submit.** You'll see their **Matri ID** and temporary password — note it down for them.
5. Help them complete the rest of their profile (or log a follow-up to do it later).

### SOP 4 — Record an offline payment (cash / GPay / NEFT)
1. Find the member → on their row click **Assign Plan** (opens **Change Membership Plan**).
2. Confirm it's the right member in the **User Found** card.
3. Choose the **Plan** they paid for (Silver / Gold / Diamond / Diamond Plus).
4. Choose the **Payment method** — GPay, Cash (branch), NEFT, PhonePe, Razorpay, Cheque, etc.
5. Add a short **reason / note** (e.g. "Cash received at branch, receipt #123").
6. **Save.** Their membership activates and the payment shows in **Payment History**.
> You can record payments, but you **cannot** change plan *prices* — that's the owner's job. If a price looks wrong, tell the owner.

## 🟢 Golden rules

**Always OK to do:** view & edit members, approve, add notes, register walk-ins, record payments, search, help members complete profiles.

**Ask the owner first / don't touch:**
- ❌ **Deleting** a member, or **Banning** anyone (use **Suspend** for a temporary hold instead).
- ❌ Anything under **Settings** (you won't see most of it anyway).
- ❌ **Refunds**, changing **plan prices**, **coupons**.

**Member privacy is everything.** Phone numbers, photos, and family details are confidential. Never share member data, screenshot it, or take it out of the office. Every action you take is logged automatically.

---

## 🆘 The four "member states" (so you use the right one)

| Action | Use it for | Reversible? |
|---|---|---|
| **Deactivate** | Member wants a temporary break | ✅ Activate |
| **Suspend** | Temporary penalty / under review | ✅ Unsuspend |
| **Ban** | Serious abuse — *ask the owner first* | (permanent) |
| **Delete** | Fake / spam / duplicate, or member asked to be removed | ✅ Restore from the **Deleted** tab |

**When unsure, ask. It's always better to ask than to guess.** 🙂
